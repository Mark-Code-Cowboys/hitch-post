# Hitch Post Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** (set at publication)
**Publisher:** Code Cowboys LLC
**Published at:** https://code-cowboys.com/privacy/hitchpost

Hitch Post is a personal log of campgrounds and RV sites. Our privacy
policy is short because the app is built so that we *cannot* see your
data.

## The short version

Everything you record in Hitch Post — campgrounds, sites, visits,
costs, notes, photos, your rig's setup — is stored **only on your
device**. Hitch Post has no accounts, no analytics, and no
advertising. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any
personal data. We could not hand your log to anyone even if asked,
because we never have it.

## Page and receipt scanning

The notebook import and receipt scanner photograph your paper pages
and read them **on your device** (Google ML Kit's bundled, offline
text recognition). Page images and everything read from them stay on
your phone. Nothing is uploaded — there is nowhere to upload it to.

## Camera and photos

Camera and photo-library access is used only when you scan pages or
receipts or attach a photo to a visit. Images you capture are stored
locally with the rest of your log.

## Location and the pin map

Hitch Post requests **no location permission** and never reads your
device's location. A campground has coordinates only if you drop its
pin on the map yourself, and pins are stored locally like everything
else.

The optional pin map draws its background from OpenStreetMap, a public
map service. When you open it, your device fetches map imagery for the
area shown — the same kind of request any web page makes — and that is
the app's **only** network use besides store purchases. None of your
log is included in those requests, and the map never loads unless you
open it.

## Sharing, export, and backup

CSV export and the backup file are created only when you tap the
button, and go only where you send them via your phone's share sheet.
Restoring a backup reads a file you choose. We are never in that path.

## Purchases

Hitch Post Pro is sold through Google Play / the App Store. The stores
process payment and tell the app only "this purchase is owned." We
receive no payment details and keep no purchase records of our own.

## Children

Hitch Post does not knowingly collect data from anyone — including
children — because it collects no data at all.

## Changes

If a future version ever changes what leaves your device, this policy
and its effective date will change with it, and the app will say so
before anything new happens.
